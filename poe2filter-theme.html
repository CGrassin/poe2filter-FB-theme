Filter tool for Path of Exile 1→ Path of Exile 2

poe2filter FilterBlade Theme

Repaint your poe2filter
in FilterBlade colors

This tool takes a filter built on poe2filter.com and restyles it with FilterBlade’s look (tier colors, icons, and effects). It only changes the look. The items' visibility and tiering are not affected.

To use it configure and download your filter on poe2filter.com, then load it below.

1

Load your filter

Drop your .filter file here
or click to select a file.
2

What to repaint

Equipment (weapons, armour, jewellery, flasks & charms) is not changed. Only the item categories below are restyled. Turn any of them off to keep the poe2filter styling.

Advanced settings

Anything you leave off keeps your poe2filter setting.

Load a filter to begin.
3

Install it in Path of Exile 2

Move the downloaded .filter file into your game's filter folder:

%userprofile%\Documents\My Games\Path of Exile 2

Then in-game open Options → Game, pick your filter from the Item Filter dropdown, and click the refresh icon to apply. You can also jump straight to this folder from that menu using the folder icon next to the dropdown.

Theme preview

The tiers correspond to the poe2filter tiers, shown in the FilterBlade color it will be repainted to.

Runs in your browser, your filter is not uploaded. Last updated on 12.07.2026. Made by Charles Grassin.
