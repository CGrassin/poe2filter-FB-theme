poe1filter FilterBlade Theme

Repaint your poe1filter
in FilterBlade colors

This tool takes a filter built on poe1filter.com and restyles it with FilterBlade’s look (tier colors, icons, and effects). It only changes the look. The items' visibility and tiering are not affected.

To use it configure and download your filter on poe1filter.com, then load it below.

1

Load your filter

Drop your .filter file here
or click to select a file.
2

What to repaint

Each category below is repainted in FilterBlade colours including Equipment (weapons, armour, jewellery & flasks). Turn any category off to keep its original poe1filter styling. It only ever changes the look; it never changes what shows, hides, or how items are tiered.

Advanced settings

Anything you leave off keeps your poe1filter setting.

Load a filter to begin.
3

Install it in Path of Exile

Move the downloaded .filter file into your game's filter folder:

%userprofile%\Documents\My Games\Path of Exile

Then in-game open Options → UI, scroll down, and pick your filter from the List of Item Filters dropdown. Click the reload icon to apply after each update.

Theme preview

The tiers correspond to the poe1filter tiers, shown in the FilterBlade colour they'll be repainted to. Uniques, maps and gems use their own tier names.

Runs in your browser, your filter is not uploaded. Last updated on 26.07.2026. Made by Charles Grassin.
